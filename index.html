How a project runs

Four stages, in this order, every time. You know what you're getting before any money changes hands.

01

Scoping call

Twenty minutes, free, no obligation. We go through your target, what you already have, and what a useful answer would look like. If computational work isn't the right spend for you right now, I'll say so.

02

Written proposal

You get scope, methods, deliverables, timeline and a fixed price in writing, plus an NDA if you want one. Nothing starts until you approve it and the deposit clears.

03

Execution with checkpoints

I run the pipeline and send a short progress update at each milestone — not silence for three weeks followed by a surprise. If early results suggest a change of direction, you hear it immediately.

04

Delivery and handover

Full report, figures, raw output files and scripts. Then a walkthrough call so you can explain the work yourself — to a supervisor, a reviewer, or an investor. One revision round is included.

Before you ask

Is my target and my data kept confidential?

Yes. I sign an NDA before receiving anything sensitive, and I'm happy to use yours rather than mine. Your structures, compound sets and results are never reused, shared, or included in my own publications or portfolio without your written permission.

Who owns the results?

You do. All output files, scripts, figures and reports are yours outright on final payment. If the work leads to a publication, authorship or acknowledgement is whatever we agree in the proposal — some clients want me listed, some don't, both are fine.

Will you write my thesis for me?

No. I'll run your computations, prepare figures, and write up methods and results for a manuscript with authorship or acknowledgement agreed in advance — that's normal scientific collaboration. What I won't do is write work that gets submitted as your own unaided thesis, because that's misconduct at your institution and puts my own academic name at risk. Plenty of legitimate help sits on this side of that line.

What if the results are negative?

You still pay, and you still get the full report. A screening campaign that finds nothing worth pursuing is a real result that saves you months of wet-lab work — and I'll tell you honestly when that's what happened rather than dressing up weak hits.

Can you guarantee the compounds will work experimentally?

No, and neither can anyone else. Computational methods rank and prioritise; they don't prove activity. What I can guarantee is that the methodology is sound, the protocols are validated, and the results are reproducible from the files I give you.

Do you work with students and small academic labs?

Yes, and that's a large part of the work. If you're a student paying out of a limited grant, say so on the enquiry form — there's an academic rate, and I'd rather scope the project down to what you can afford than turn you away.

How do I pay from outside India?

International bank transfer, Wise, or PayPal, invoiced in USD or EUR. Indian clients can pay by UPI or bank transfer in INR, at the reduced institutional rate shown when you switch the currency in the header. Fifty percent up front, the balance on delivery.
